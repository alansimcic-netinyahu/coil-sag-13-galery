🌍 SDG 13 / Nova Gorica
SDG 13 Climate Action

Small actions.
Cooler future.

Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts. We look at what this means for Nova Gorica, a young border city on the Soča river.

1. The problem

Burning coal, oil and gas adds CO₂ to the air. The planet warms, and weather becomes more extreme.

2. Our city

Nova Gorica has hot summers, strong rain and heavy traffic across the border with Gorizia.

3. Our answer

Cleaner transport, more trees, efficient buildings and smart daily habits. Every pledge counts.

What is happening here.

Scientists and weather services report that the climate is warming. Here is how it shows up around Nova Gorica.

🌡️ Hotter summers

Heat waves are more frequent. Dark roofs and concrete squares trap heat, so city centres feel hotter than the countryside.

🌧️ Drought and downpours

The Soča and Vipava region can swing between dry spells and sudden heavy rain, which stresses rivers, farms and drains.

🔥 Wildfires on the Karst

In summer 2022 large wildfires burned on the Karst plateau on both sides of the Slovenia–Italy border.

🚗 Transport emissions

Many daily trips are made by car. Better trains, buses and bike lanes between Nova Gorica and Gorizia can cut them.

🤝 One border city

Nova Gorica and Gorizia share the Transalpina Square and were European Capital of Culture together in 2025. Climate action works best when both sides cooperate.

🏠 Energy at home

Heating and cooling buildings uses a lot of energy. Insulation, heat pumps and rooftop solar reduce it.

How we can improve.

Ideas for people, schools and the city. Tick your pledges to fill the meter.

🚲 Move smarter

Walk, cycle or take the train. Safer cross-border bike routes and frequent trains make this easy.

🌳 Plant shade

Trees on squares and school yards cool the air and soak up rain.

☀️ Use clean energy

Solar panels on roofs of schools and homes, plus better insulation.

♻️ Waste less

Repair, reuse, share and recycle. Avoid food waste.

📣 Spread the word

Talk to family and friends, and ask local leaders for climate plans.

📚 Keep learning

Follow science, not myths. Check sources.
